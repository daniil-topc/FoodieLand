Fresh, comforting, and full of color, this Japanese-inspired fried rice combines fluffy rice, tender vegetables, and soft-boiled eggs. It’s a satisfying meal that comes together quickly, making it perfect for lunch or a relaxed dinner. Finish with fresh herbs and a splash of soy sauce to bring all the flavors together.

Directions

  1. 1

    Prepare the rice and vegetables

    Break up the chilled rice with your hands so every grain cooks evenly. Slice the vegetables into small pieces and keep all the ingredients close to the stove—the frying happens quickly.

    For the best texture, use rice that has cooled completely. Freshly cooked rice contains more moisture and can become soft instead of lightly crisp.

  2. 2

    Cook the eggs and aromatics

    Warm the sesame oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the green onions and vegetables, then stir-fry until fragrant and just tender. Move everything to one side and gently scramble the eggs in the open space.

  3. 3

    Fry, season, and serve

    Add the rice and toss it through the vegetables and eggs. Pour in the soy sauce, sweet chili sauce, and rice vinegar. Fry for another two to three minutes, then serve hot with fresh herbs and soft-boiled eggs.
